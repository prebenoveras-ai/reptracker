        Calibrate the board

        Every sensor reads gravity slightly differently on each axis (yours read 1.04 g on one side and 1.00 g on another). One minute of calibration makes speed and range much more accurate, especially on the wrist.

        1. Connect the board.
        2. Tap Start, then lay the board still on a table on each of its 6 sides in turn (chip up, chip down, and each of the 4 edges, leaning it against something).
        3. Each side is captured automatically after about 2 s of stillness.

        Feedback

        Velocity loss is how much slower your latest rep is than the fastest early rep. Around 20% keeps 3 to 5 reps in reserve on big lifts. 40% is close to failure.

        Exercise recognition

        RepTracker learns what your exercises look like from your saved recordings, then suggests the exercise when a set doesn't match the one typed in.
